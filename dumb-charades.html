How to Play Dumb Charades – Step by Step

1. Pick a mode and count
Choose Easy for family and easy, Medium for Bollywood fans, or Hard for movie experts. Select how many movies per round.

2. Click Generate
Hit Generate and Bollywood movie titles appear instantly. The actor reads privately while everyone else looks away.

3. Use Full Screen
Switch to Full Screen so only the actor sees the movie title as they prepare to act.

4. Show the word count
Hold up fingers for the number of words in the title. Point to each finger as you act each word.

5. Act without speaking
Use body language and gestures only. Tug your ear for "sounds like." Use classic dumb charades signals.

6. Set a 30-second timer
One round equals 30 seconds. A correct guess scores a point. If time runs out, the other team can steal.

7. Rotate and keep going
Take turns so everyone acts at least twice. Click Generate for unlimited fresh Bollywood movie titles.
