🤲 Classic Movie Charades Hand Signals

Before you start acting, make sure your whole group knows these classic hand signals. Learning them takes 60 seconds and makes the game 10 times faster and more fun.

🎬

It's a Movie

Spin your hand in front of your eye like a film reel. Do this first before acting.

✋

Number of Words

Hold up fingers to show how many words are in the movie title.

☝️

Which Word

Hold up one finger for the first word, two for the second, and so on.

👂

Sounds Like

Cup your hand behind your ear. Then act a word that rhymes or sounds similar.

🤏

Small Word

Pinch fingers close together. Signal for short words like "the", "a", "of".

✅

Correct Guess

Point at whoever guessed correctly to confirm they got it right.

🏆 Tips for Acting Out Movie Titles in Charades

Always Signal "Movie" First

Spin your hand in front of your eye like a film reel before anything else. This single signal tells the team exactly what category they are guessing.

Show the Word Count

Hold up the number of fingers matching the word count in the title. Then point to one finger at a time as you act each word.

Act the Most Iconic Scene

If the title itself is hard to mime, act the film's most famous scene instead. The team will often recognise the film from the scene even without word-by-word clues.

Use "Sounds Like" for Hard Words

Cup your ear for sounds like, then mime a simpler rhyming word. Creative phonetic links unlock otherwise impossible titles.

Skip Small Words

Pinch your fingers close together and shake your head to signal a small word like "the", "a", "of", or "and."

Play by Genre for Themed Nights

For Bollywood movie nights, horror movie nights, or Disney evenings, stick to one category throughout.
