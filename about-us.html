Our Story

Charade Generator started from a simple annoyance: most charades word lists online are either a single static page you have to scroll through by hand, or a generator so generic it repeats the same twenty words all night. This site is independently built and maintained by Syed Ismail, a web developer who wanted a charades tool that actually adapts to who's playing.

That's why the site isn't one generator — it's five. The main Character Charades tool lets you filter by superheroes, movies, fairy tales, video games, or classic TV and pulls a fresh, non-repeating character each round, with a built-in 30-second timer for group play. Christmas Charades and Islamic Charades are curated, ready-to-print lists for holiday nights and family or madrasa gatherings, organized by theme rather than randomized, since those settings usually call for a set you can plan around rather than one you leave to chance. Reverse Charades flips the format entirely — the whole group acts while one player guesses — for parties and younger kids who'd rather not act solo.

"I built the version of this game I kept wishing existed — one that actually fits the group you're playing with, instead of a single one-size-fits-all word list."

— Syed Ismail, creator of Charade Generator

Everything here is free with no account required. Next up: expanding the Character Charades pool with more video game and classic TV entries, based on what people have asked for so far.
