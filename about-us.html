About Charade Generator

CharadeGenerator is a free website for playing charades. It has random prompt cards and themed pages for families, classrooms, parties and community events.

Who Runs This Site

CharadeGenerator was created and is run by web developer Syed Ismail. He built it because most online party-game tools are cluttered, ask you to install an app or create an account, or give you the same short word list over and over. The goal is a simple page that opens fast on a phone and works on a TV during game night.

You can reach him through the Contact page or on Instagram.

What You Will Find Here

Each page is built for a different kind of game night, so you can pick the one that fits your group:

General Charades

Random action-and-style cards (like "Skip like a penguin") with a 30-second timer. Good for any age.

Character Charades

Famous fictional characters from superheroes, movies, fairy tales, video games and classic TV.

Christmas Charades

Festive prompts for holiday parties and multi-generational gatherings.

Islamic Charades

Halal prompts on Salah, Ramadan, Hajj and good manners, plus a madrasa lesson plan.

Reverse Charades

A team format where everyone acts and one person guesses. Great for large groups.

How We Write and Check Prompts

  • Actability. A good charades prompt can be shown with the body. We prefer concrete actions, scenes and recognizable characters over abstract ideas like "bureaucracy".
  • Family-friendly. Every page is meant for mixed-age groups. No prompt is meant to embarrass or mock anyone.
  • Respect for themed content. The Christmas and Islamic pages are written with attention to the traditions they cover. The Islamic page, for example, avoids acting out Prophets and Angels.
  • Corrections welcome. If you spot an error, a prompt that does not fit your community, or a missing category, tell us through the Contact page and we will review it.
