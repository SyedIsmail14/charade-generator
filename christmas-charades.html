Hosting a Holiday Game Night

Many board games leave young children or older relatives out because of long rulebooks. Charades needs only imagination, and everyone knows the imagery of the season. Players can focus on humor, timing and silent problem solving.

Agree on a few ground rules before you begin. Actors stay completely silent: no whispering, no humming carols and no sound effects. Everything is shown through facial expressions, posture and mime. Decide beforehand whether real objects in the room (the tree, stockings, presents) may be used. Most groups choose not to.

Formats for Family Gatherings

Classic Team Duel

Two teams, 60 seconds per turn. After four rounds, unused seconds convert into bonus points.

Holiday Reverse Charades

One person guesses while four or five family members act together. It is great for shy players. See Reverse Charades for the full rules.

Stocking Stuffer Speed Run

One actor works through as many prompts from a single set as possible in 90 seconds. Each correct guess scores a point.

Tips for a Smooth Party

  • Start with Winter or Santa sets. They are the easiest to act, so everyone warms up quickly.
  • Mix the teams by age. Pair children with adults so nobody feels stuck.
  • Print a few copies. A printed list keeps phones away from the party and lets two games run at once.
  • Write your own. Add family traditions, such as "Grandma's famous fruit cake", for a personal round.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can actors use real decorations or gifts in the room?

In standard charades the actor mimes everything. Agree before the game whether real objects are allowed. Most groups choose not to use them, so the game stays about acting.

How should we handle prompts for young children?

Pair children with an adult co-actor, choose the Winter or Santa sets, and allow about 90 seconds per turn. Animal-style moves like a prancing reindeer work well.

What if a prompt isn't guessed in time?

The other team can get one chance to confer and make a single guess to steal the point. You can also just move on to the next prompt.

Can we print the prompts?

Yes. Print this page (Ctrl+P, or Command+P on Mac). Menus and tools are hidden so you get clean prompt lists.
