3. Cookies and Analytics

Cookies are small text records deposited on your device by websites you visit. Charade Generator utilizes cookies for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Functional & Session Cookies: To preserve your selected game modes, sound settings, or active full-screen layouts during an active round.
  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics 4): To evaluate user trends, measure load speeds, and identify which game categories are most popular. GA4 records anonymized, aggregated traffic data without storing individual IP addresses.
  • Advertising Cookies: Set by Google AdSense and authorized programmatic partners to manage ad delivery, prevent duplicate ad impressions, and deliver relevant promotions.

You can delete or block cookies at any time via your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that turning off all cookies may impact certain interactive UI features.
