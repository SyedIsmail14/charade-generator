🏆 Tips for Acting Out Characters in Charades

Lead With the Iconic Move

Every famous character has one move everyone knows. Spider-Man's web flick. Hulk's double-fist smash. Use it immediately — don't build up to it.

Show the Personality, Not Just the Look

Characters have personalities as much as costumes. Batman is slow, deliberate, and brooding. Sonic is twitchy and impatient.

Use Props You Already Have

Pick up a nearby object to represent a character prop. A pen becomes Hermione's wand. A bag becomes Dora's backpack.

Act the Character's Situation

Harry Potter isn't just waving a wand — he is nervously facing Voldemort. Woody is panicking because Andy is about to leave for college.

Mirror the Voice (Without Sound)

Mouth the character's exaggerated speech patterns without making noise. Homer's slow open-mouthed thinking face. Gollum's precious-obsessed expression.

Start With Category Signal

Before acting, signal the category to your team — mime a film reel for movie characters, mime a controller for video game characters.

How to Play Character Charades – Step by Step

1. Pick a mode and count
Choose how many characters you need using the number buttons. Then select a mode — All for mixed play, easy for young children, Superheroes for action fans, Movies for film lovers, or Fairy Tale for family groups.

2. Click Generate
Hit the Generate button and character charades appear instantly. Each card shows a famous character with an action or situation to act out.

3. Keep it secret
Only the actor reads the card. Use Full Screen mode and hold the phone so only you can see the prompt.

4. Signal the category first
Before acting, show your team whether it is a movie character, cartoon, video game, or fairy tale.

5. Act without speaking
Use body language, gestures, and facial expressions only. No sounds, no mouthing words, no writing in the air.

6. Set a 30-second timer
One round equals 30 seconds. A correct guess in time scores a point.

7. Rotate and repeat
Take turns clockwise so every player gets to act. Click Generate for unlimited fresh character charades.
