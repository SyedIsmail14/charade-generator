Acting Technique: How to Sell a Character in Seconds

  1. Lead with the signature move. Every well-known character has one gesture people recognize instantly — Spider-Man's web-shooting hand, the Hulk's double-fist slam. Open with it instead of building up to it.
  2. Play the personality, not just the costume. Batman moves slow and deliberate. Sonic is twitchy and impatient. The posture sells the character as much as any prop would.
  3. Borrow a nearby object as a prop. A pen becomes a wand. A cushion becomes a shield. Real objects register faster than mimed ones.
  4. Act the situation, not the pose. Harry Potter isn't just holding a wand — he's nervously facing down Voldemort. Show the moment, not just the character.
  5. Exaggerate the mouth shape without sound. A slow, open-mouthed "thinking" face reads as Homer. A tight, obsessive whisper-shape reads as Gollum — no actual sound needed.
  6. Signal the category before you start. A quick film-reel spin or game-controller mime tells your team where to start guessing before you even move.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is character charades different from regular charades?

Regular charades uses random words or phrases. Character charades restricts the pool to known people and figures — from movies, games, TV and fairy tales — so the guessing is grounded in something the whole team already recognizes.

Can I mix categories in one game?

Yes — the "All" mode above pulls from every category at once. Pick a single category instead if your group has a shared interest, like movies or video games.

Does the generator repeat characters?

The generator avoids repeats within a session until the full pool for that category has been used, so a long game session keeps producing fresh characters.

Do I need to install anything to play?

No. It runs directly in your browser on desktop or mobile — just click Generate and go. Full Screen mode is built in for group play.
