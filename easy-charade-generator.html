🏆 Tips for Playing easy Charades

Let Young easy Make Sounds

For children aged 4 to 6, allow sounds in the first few rounds to build confidence. Gradually move to silent acting as children become comfortable.

Always Encourage, Never Correct

If a child's acting is unclear, never say it is wrong. Cheer every attempt. The goal is participation and confidence, not perfect mime technique.

Use Full Screen for the Actor

Hold up the phone in full screen so only the acting child can see the prompt. This keeps the word secret naturally.

Start with 1 Card Only

For first-time players and younger children, generate just 1 charade at a time. Increase to 3 as children get comfortable.

Give a 30-Second Timer

For children aged 4 to 8, use 30 seconds instead of 60. Shorter rounds keep energy high and ensure every child gets multiple turns.

Copy and Share for Virtual Play

Use the Copy button to share generated charades via WhatsApp or Zoom chat for virtual birthday parties or remote family game nights.
