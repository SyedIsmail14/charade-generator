🏆 Tips for Playing Summer Charades

Play at the Actual Beach or Pool

Summer charades is most fun when played in the real summer setting it celebrates. Acting "applying sunscreen" while actually sitting on a beach towel, surrounded by real sand and sea, adds genuine atmosphere to every round.

Use the Heat to Your Advantage

Many summer charades involve heat and sweat — lean into it. Exaggerated fanning, dramatic wiping of brow, desperate searching for shade all communicate "hot summer day" context.

Great for Rainy Camp Days

When outdoor camp activities get cancelled due to weather, summer charades is the perfect indoor alternative that still celebrates camp culture.

Use Real Props When Available

At a barbecue or beach day, use real available props — a beach towel as a surfboard, an actual ice cream cone as a prop, real sunglasses for sunbathing charades.

Perfect for Road Trips

Summer charades works brilliantly as a car game during family road trips. Use the Copy button to read charades aloud for whoever is acting in the back seat.

Play in the Evening for Camp Mode

Camp charades feel most authentic when played in the evening, ideally near a real or imagined campfire. The golden hour lighting perfectly matches the campfire storytelling energy.
