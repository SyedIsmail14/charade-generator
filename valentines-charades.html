🏆 Tips for Playing Valentine's Charades

Pick Mode Based on Your Group

Choose easy mode for classroom parties, Couples mode for date nights, Romantic mode for mixed groups celebrating love generally, and Funny mode for Galentine's parties or singles' gatherings.

Act the Emotion First

Romance is emotional before it is physical. Lead with the feeling — nervous excitement for a first date, deep tenderness for a love letter, comedic panic for a forgotten Valentine's Day.

For Couples — Use Real Inside Jokes

Couples playing together can adapt prompts to reference their actual relationship history, making the game more meaningful and personal.

For Love Story Prompts — Use the Iconic Pose

Famous love story scenes have one defining pose. Lead with that single iconic image for instant recognition.

Galentine's Day Adaptation

For Galentine's parties celebrating friendship, focus on Funny and Traditions modes which work without requiring romantic relationship content.

Use Full Screen for Date Night

Couples can use Full Screen mode to take turns acting for each other in a private, playful date night activity.
