🏆 Tips for Playing Occupation Charades

Use the Signature Tool

Every occupation has a defining tool or prop. Mime holding a stethoscope, a hammer, a paintbrush, or a steering wheel before doing anything else. The tool often identifies the profession faster than the action alone.

Show the Specific Movement

Generic "working" gestures are too vague. Show the precise, specific movement unique to that job — a surgeon's careful steady hands versus a builder's forceful hammering.

Use for Career Day Events

Generate prompts from specific categories matching your career day theme — Medical mode for healthcare day, Creative mode for arts week, Trades mode for vocational education events.

Discuss the Real Job After Guessing

After each correct guess, briefly discuss what that job actually involves day to day. This turns entertainment into genuine career education.

Show the Work Environment

If the action alone is unclear, add environmental context — crouch low for an underground miner, reach up high for an aircraft engineer, hunch over a desk for an accountant.

Encourage Trade Career Respect

When playing Trades mode, take time to genuinely celebrate these professions as skilled, valuable careers.
