🏆 Tips for Playing Bollywood Charades

Signal the Film First

Spin your hand like a film reel before acting — this tells your team it is a Bollywood movie and narrows their thinking to Hindi cinema immediately.

Use the Iconic Pose

Every Bollywood film has one defining pose. SRK's arms spread wide, Gabbar's menacing crouch, the Dangal wrestling stance. Lead with that single image.

Beat the 30-Second Timer

The timer auto-starts the moment you hit Generate. Jump straight into the iconic pose — no warming up. The clock waits for nobody!

For Songs — Use the Dance Move

The signature dance move of a Bollywood song communicates it faster than any plot clue. Chaiyya Chaiyya's rooftop stance is instantly recognisable.

Show the Emotion

Bollywood is ALL about emotion. The heartbreak of Devdas, the joy of Jai Ho, the sacrifice of Mother India. Lean into the feeling — it communicates fast.

Best for Desi Gatherings

Play after dinner at Eid, Diwali, mehndi or any family gathering. Requires no equipment — just the phone, and the shared Bollywood love every Desi family has.
